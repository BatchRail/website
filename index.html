The Problem

Exact settlement adds up at volume

x402 volume is real. On tickets around $0.02–$0.03, a $0.001 per-settle facilitator fee is a few percent of GMV — not a third — but it compounds across millions of calls. Batch-settlement exists; tooling and ops are why it is not the default.

Why batch isn’t winning yet

  • Counterparty risk blocks adoption
  • Tooling friction and accounting overhead
  • No durable channel state out of the box
  • Background claim / settle / refund is operationally heavy

What BatchRail changes

  • Hosted batch facilitator endpoints (x402 v2 compatible)
  • Managed channel claim / settle loops
  • Open examples you can run locally on Base Sepolia
  • Path to production hosting for merchants and agents
The Solution

Batch-settlement as the default

Buyer deposits once into escrow, signs cumulative off-chain vouchers, seller claims in bulk. One onchain settle can cover many paid HTTP calls. Public demo is Base Sepolia only.
