The Problem

Exact settlement is eating the margin

x402 volume is real. Average transaction sizes of ~$0.023–$0.031 mean a $0.001 facilitator fee consumes 32–43% of GMV.

🔴 Why batch isn’t winning yet

  • Counterparty risk blocks adoption
  • Tooling friction and accounting overhead
  • No durable channel state out of the box
  • Background claim / settle / refund is operationally heavy
  • Merchants must lock capital or take credit risk

🟢 What BatchRail changes

  • Hosted batch facilitator endpoints (x402 v2 compatible)
  • Managed Channel Manager as a Service
  • Real-time Grok risk scoring & credit limits
  • Bot swarm for monitoring, claims, reconciliation
  • Optional LP-backed float so agents stay capital-light
The Solution

Batch-settlement as the default

Buyer deposits once into escrow, signs cumulative off-chain vouchers, seller claims in bulk. BatchRail runs the hard parts so merchants capture nearly all GMV.
